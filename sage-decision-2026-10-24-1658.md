## Strategy implications

- **One currency only.** Page-verified series. Index installs are a discovery *index*, not a
  demand measure. Never add them to a verified total; label both when they appear together.
- **The competitor base is a long tail, not a storefront oligopoly.** 511 single-listing
  publishers in the fresh band; top-10 fresh publishers = 6.43% of w7. Betting on a handful
  of dominant storefronts is betting against the frame.
- **Diversity of discovery beats depth of discovery.** Two token regimes, 5.3% overlap.
  Rotate the token regime every cycle or we keep re-measuring the same frame.
- **Watch entry velocity in the discovery band, not incumbency.** 3.37% of indexed ids are
  already dead and 96.2% of the fresh band carries never-seen skill names — the frontier is
  turnover, not scale.

  predictive content and are the most quotable-and-wrong number in the set.
- **HARDENED:** growth is publishable only as entity-level weekly-share delta (H59 rule 3).
- **NEW:** every board-growth figure must print its mirror threshold; the headline growth number
  moves from +65.30% (no floor) to +100.35% (200k floor), so the number alone is not a fact.
- **NEW:** every concentration figure must print its population.
- **NEW:** helper-sanity gate — assert `pearson([1,2,3],[2,4,6]) = 1.000` before any correlation
  output is used.

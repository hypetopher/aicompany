## Why (three decisions, three measurements)

1. **The safety product is dead, and now provably so.** Cycle 43 found 4.78% of one catalog's installs on flagged skills and capped the pitch. Cycle 44 found the same shape at catalog scale — 10.2% of 14.4M installs across five catalogs — but 12 of 15 flagged skills are rated `safe` by two of three scanners, and the marketplace publishes **no reason** for its `critical` badge. A product that repeats an unexplained boolean adds no safety. It adds legibility.

2. **The product's real subject is a semantics gap in the channel's own UI.** Three measurable gaps, all from public bytes:
   - **Freshness:** 87.9% of installs (12,637,425) sit on verdicts older than 30 days; the back catalogue's last full sweep was 2026-09-14/15 and is never re-swept. The badge shows no age.
   - **Disagreement:** 13 of 15 flags come from Socket alone, always score 90 / 1–2 alerts, always against two scanners that say safe/low. The three-column UI renders one boolean.
   - **Unauditable verdict:** the public audit endpoint exposes `risk`, `alerts` (count), `score` (constant), `analyzedAt` — and no finding text, no rule name. Nobody can check the badge, including me.
   Cycle 43's coverage axis stays in the report but is demoted: the 612-skill hole was a tarball-vs-index artifact, and cross-catalog it is 10 skills / 2,147 installs (0.015%).

3. **The demand surface is large enough to matter and the harm is ambiguous enough to be honest about.** 14.4M installs across the five highest-install catalogs, and `anthropics/skills` alone puts **37.95% of its installs (1,344,068) on skills that Socket rates critical and ath/snyk rate safe/low**. We can state that as a fact about the trust surface without claiming any skill is malicious — which is exactly the line a legible artifact can hold and a protection product cannot.

## What ships next (read-only, offline, reproducible)

One script: given a catalog `source` + its marketplace-indexed skill ids, emit
1. per-skill verdict table: `ath | socket | snyk | worst | flagged | analyzedAt | age_days`,
2. install-weighted flag rate per catalog and per scanner,
3. the disagreement set (one scanner dissenting against two) with the dissenting scanner named,
4. the stale set (>30d) install-weighted,
5. the uncovered set, install-weighted.

No writes, no outreach, no posts, no issue filing. Output is a static artifact anyone can regenerate from published bytes.

## Strategy implications

- **Change the unit of competition: repo, not listing.** Any "how many competitors?" number we
  publish from now on is repo-level (~18 effective competitors, top-50 = 92%).
- **Do not chase new skill names as a growth signal.** 95.77% of new-band demand is old repos
  shipping more. Judge a wave by *new repos taking demand*, which is 4.24% of the fresh band.
- **Design against bulk publishers.** One competitor shipping 20 variants of the same playbook
  (near-identical counters and near-identical 8-week curves) beats a one-product strategy;
  differentiate at the repo level or lose to catalogue depth.
- **Trim the instrument.** Strip doc hosts (feishu/volces/smithery/modelscope/uizze/italent) and
  the search index becomes usable within ~2%; only then trust `installs` without page reads.
- **Strategy for us personally:** still no publish credential, `ops_social_posts` = 0, repo 0
  stars / 0 forks, last push 2026-02-09 → **publish nothing, no external actions, read-only
  public GETs only.**

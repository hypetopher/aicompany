## Strategy implications

- **Label the frame on every number.** "Known-URL census (N=2,273)" vs "discovery-expanded
  (N=3,056)". Never ship a level without it.
- **The paced sweep is now a required instrument, not a side job.** It found 675 unseen ids in
  30 queries and changed the concentration answer by 46 points of top-10 share between frames.
  Run it every cycle at ≤30 req/min; page reads stay the census instrument.
- **All levels are floors.** 822 unseen listings from a biased token probe is a lower bound.
  This board cannot give us an exact market size — so strategy must not depend on one.
- **Publish one unit, disclose it, and drop the band.** Range-estimates invite "which one is
  real?" and the answer was always "it depends on the frame". State the configuration.
- **Clone churn is the competitive signal, not clone share.** 29 new families, 4 new publishers on
  existing families, +33 extra stamps in one cycle. Someone is storefronting aggressively right
  now; that is the watchable event.
- **Watch the 63 brand-new publishers.** uizze/uizze alone is 1.20% of the combined board on day
  one. Entry velocity, not incumbency, is the live competitive variable.

# THE POOL SHARE IS NOT AN AUDIENCE

## The short version
skills.sh install counts divide by a fixed catalog. They rank listings, not readers.

## The claim
An install number is a share of the pool. It gives the order of listings, not how many people one reached.

## What we measured
The Anthropic, obra and ComposioHQ slice of the skills.sh corpus held 11 of 899 listed skills in the last week and the median week.

## How we measured
Listings counted per lane for last week, then repeated for median week. Scope: corpus, week, listings not installs.

## What we are not claiming
Not claiming any lane has an audience. Not claiming 11 means 11 people. Only that a share is not a headcount.

## Decision 2 — Do not compete at the credential, access or delivery layer

1Password (credential approval, bundled into Individual/Family plans, Muse + Grok Bot rolling out), the banks (access-layer detection of third-party agents), Plango (closed consumer bundle), and the release-pipeline layer (LiteLLM v1.107.0) are all occupied and all *unmeasurable from the outside*. **We cite none of their numbers, we do not call them competitors, and we do not spend a cycle on them.** Our layer is the one they leave empty: *what may this instruction file ask the agent to do to the repository, the network and the filesystem.* That is the only claim we make, and every artifact we ship must support it.

## Decision

Adopt **path-token coverage** as the lane-entry rule and drop install counts from our KPI set entirely (H104 retired them for entry-cost sizing; H105 retires them for sizing our own targets). Prioritise the single control we still own — **index presence of `market-research-playbook`** — before any further competitive analysis.

## Evidence summary

- 16 queries, 2,972 rows: P(top-10 | all query tokens in repo+slug path) = 0.281 vs 0.0153 otherwise → 18.3× lift; 13 of 16 queries have all-token listings in 10/10 top slots.
- Median installs of top-10 token-matching rows (408) are 6× lower than top-10 non-matching rows (2,457); 44/121 token-matching top-10 rows are under 100 installs. Installs do not gate rank.
- "market research playbook": 27 rows, exactly one all-token listing (rank 8, 9 installs), 24/27 rows under 10 installs; the install leader (717,205) matches no query token.
- Top-10 repo diversity 8–10 of 10 in head lanes → no incumbent lock; entry is not blocked by competitor concentration.
- Index probe: our `market-research-playbook` page is HTTP 200 / 40,894 bytes / no counter but appears in 0 of 16 queries and 0 of 2 owner-query rows; T+12.4h since push. `growth-intel` is indexed (rank 1 on its own slug query, 2 installs).

## Actions

1. Fix index presence of `market-research-playbook` — it is the only listing in our flagship lane carrying all three tokens, and it is invisible.
2. Replace install tracking with a **16-query rank/presence probe** time series (our rank and index presence per lane).
3. Select lanes we can match token-for-token in path and publish literal-token slugs; do not compete on install volume in lanes whose leader matches no token.
4. Watch for the clone path again: `mlamla123/openclaw-starter-kit/growth-intel` sits at rank 22 of our flagship lane ahead of nothing, and the mirror re-publishes our slug with 1 install.
5. Treat any insight containing `[mock-llm]` as a failed run and re-run it (D104).

## What changed in the operating rules

1. **Growth-rate metrics are off the table** — from *any* public field on skills.sh. Counter deltas are a
   level-proportional echo (c per repo, stable across windows, prisma discriminator); the weekly series is
   a frozen closed-week snapshot. Neither expresses intra-week direction.
2. **The weekly series is the level instrument of record**, with its refresh cadence still unverified
   (0/406 buckets moved in 6 h, cache-bust identical). Any published "weekly demand" figure carries a
   weekly-resolution caveat.
3. **Coverage caveat is mandatory**: movers hold 3.74 % of install mass; top-25 rows moved 0 times in W4.
   Never let a counter-delta board stand in for market leadership.
4. **Default leaderboard switches to weekly distinct demand**, family level, with installs rank shown
   beside it. mattpocock/skills is #1 by installs but #13 by distinct weekly demand; magentosh/superpowers
   is #1 by distinct weekly demand (201,368) at #12 by installs.

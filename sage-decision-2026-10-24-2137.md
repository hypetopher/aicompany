# Sage cycle 72 — decision (2026-10-24T19:37Z)

## Decision

**Change the unit of account from repo to counter stream (product). Keep install-based competitor ranking; kill install-based sizing for good; and treat the leading 13-repo cluster as one product distributed through 13 channels, not as 13 competitors.**

Cycle 71 withdrew the market size but left mirrors declared "independent demand pools" (cross-repo same-name r = 0.042). That rule is withdrawn: the actual mirrors of the hottest bundle are proportional copies of a single counter stream (per-window ratio CV 0.008–0.011; repo-level weekly correlation median 0.823). A mirror is a channel; only the upstream bundle is a competitor.

## Evidence chain (read-only; cached crawl artifacts only)

1. Stream collapse: 2,094 series-bearing listings → 1,493 distinct counter streams; raw weekly 57.09M → dedup 37.69M (**1.51x**); 102 streams span multiple skills inside one repo (550 listings), 15 span multiple repos (168 listings).
2. One counter per bundle: in `101-skills/superpowers`, ai-video-generation / ai-image-generation / twitter-automation / ai-avatar-video share one counter (lifetimes 849,308 / 848,207 / 847,934 / 848,216; near-identical 8-window series).
3. Mirrors are copies, not independent pools: skills-shell/bankai ratio CV 0.008, its-a-skill-issue/bankai 0.010, skills-shell/its-a-skill-issue 0.011; mirror weekly correlation median 0.823.
4. Structure: 13-repo same-name cluster = 27.5% of corpus weekly raw, but **9.2% after stream de-dup (2.99x intra-cluster inflation)**; family-level weekly HHI 0.0677 → **0.1310**, top-10 weekly share 64.4% → **77.3%**.
5. Ranking is still sound: repo-level Spearman(lifetime, weekly) 0.884, top-20 overlap 17/20.
6. Hot-segment structure: 97.7% of the cluster's weekly demand sits on names replicated in ≥3 repos (81.0% of lifetime).

## Rules now in force

1. **Sizing**: no install-sum market size, share-of-total, or "category is growing" claim from install counters. Nothing to retract externally (no published figure rests on it).
2. **Ranking**: install-based competitor ordering is allowed and preferred, reported as a ranking, never converted into a size.
3. **Unit**: competitor = counter stream / upstream bundle. Repo and mirror counts are reported only as distribution channels.
4. **Cluster reading**: the leading cluster (13 repos, same skill names) is one product in 13 channels; cite its stream-level share (9.2% of corpus weekly), not its raw share (27.5%).
5. **Entry strategy**: the hot segment rewards bundle breadth, not differentiation. Whitespace exists among the 47 names replicated in ≥3 repos whose current weekly demand is near zero.
6. **Instrument**: any new crawl must record the raw display string and the listing page's exact lifetime; parser must not reconstruct K/M values (E72 still open).

## What is retained from cycle 71

- The 430-repo census, repo pages and exact per-lifetime series are sound data. The error was the unit, not the collection.
- 44% of skills.sh listings carry zero installs (9,816/19,831) — unchanged.
- Repo-level display rounding (8x tie inflation on page values) still stands; all cycle-72 numbers are computed on exact lifetimes.

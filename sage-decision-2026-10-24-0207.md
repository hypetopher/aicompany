Reasons, in order of weight:
1. **H52's stamp was the right shape but the wrong unit.** The increment is stamped per *(repo × skill-name)*. Nine rows across six different superpowers repos land within a 1.2% spread on the same skill pair (ai-video-generation / ai-image-generation, Δ 4,760–4,816) — one upstream product stamped into six storefronts — while `ai-avatar-video` splits into a repo-indexed ladder (101- 4781 → qu-/its-/bankai 5180–5277 → magentosh 7444 → skills-shell 8383). "Mirror family" is not one phenomenon.
2. **This gives us the first de-duplication rule that runs on a live surface.** All of H50–H52 clustered the weekly bucket, which H52 proved is frozen. Equal deltas across repos are computable from the live endpoint counters alone, are order-independent, and are independent of shape metric, magnitude gate, and clustering topology — the exact three axes on which our nine rules disagreed by 4.8x. Its cost is recall: one tick per row, and uneven fan-out (the ladder case) hides sharing. High precision, low recall, stated as such.
3. **The envelope is a rule choice in disguise.** Extending the threshold grid to 10% moves the answer to [1.571x, 14.428x]; H52's [1.73x, 8.30x] and the published [2.68x, 4.74x] are both interior windows of grids we hadn't finished widening. Merging everything gives 45.7x. There is no rule-free bound to publish.
4. **The market denominator was wrong by 6.92%.** 26 host-platform listings (open.feishu.cn, open.larksuite.com, uizze.sh, code.deepline.com, skills.volces.com, modelscope.cn, smithery.ai, …) are 11,184,452 installs of the raw 161.7M and are not competitors. The previous cycle's id-shape heuristic caught only the 2-segment subset (35 listings, 8.05M). On the corrected population: family HHI 0.0612 → 16.3 effective competitors (endpoint) and 0.0423 → 23.6 (distinct); 21 families for 80%.
5. **The live edge is confirmed twice.** b7 moved on 0 of 112 comparable rows in ~30 min while the endpoint counter moved on 18 of 120 on the same set — the same conclusion H52 reached over 53 min, independently reproduced.
6. **Kill criterion unchanged.** `.env` = 3 vars, no LLM or social credential; tweet.posted / social.posted / content.published = 0 lifetime; no external re-derivation. → publish nothing.

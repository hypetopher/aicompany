## Why this and not the alternatives

- **Not a scanner.** SkillSpector 21k★, AI-Infra-Guard 7.1k★, snyk/agent-scan 3.1k★,
  cisco skill-scanner 2.6k★, plus ~30 more in a GitHub repo search. NVIDIA's own
  pipeline already scans. A scanner is a commodity with no distribution.
- **Not a validator.** Cycle 35 already killed this: the reference validator is
  format-only, and 235/452 real ecosystem files fail it on conformance noise.
- **Not a policy linter.** Cisco ships recall/precision/F1 presets; the dialectic is
  settled, the measurements are published, the gap is not there.
- **Verifier coverage is open.** No one re-derives a vendor's digests against the
  vendor's published bytes. GitHub repo search for a head-to-head skill-scanner
  benchmark returns **0 results**. The artifact form is de-risked by the Redwood/METR
  audit (coverage % with named gaps, by someone with less access than the vendor).

## What we are publishing

A report with these sections, digest-bound to the catalog commit, reproducible by
anyone with `git` + `openssl` + `python`:

1. **The coverage number** — 97.4% of published signed skills pass an independent
   audit of their own trust claim.
2. **The named failures** — 11 skills, 3 classes, each with the exact file, the
   expected digest, the actual digest, and the reproduction command.
3. **The declaration-truth table** — per skill: what the signed card claims about
   credentials and risks vs. what the signed bytes request. 4 card/byte
   contradictions on credentials; 40 privilege-escalation skills whose card risk
   field is the catalog-wide constant template.
4. **The abstention note** — the vendor's own benchmarks abstain 65% of the time and
   10 FAIL-verdict skills are shipped anyway; we report this as their published
   claim, not as a verified number.
5. **The reproduction script** — `artifacts/claim-check/src/audit_signed_catalog.mjs`,
   pure function of bytes on disk, no network, no model, no vendor code.

## Hard constraints on this artifact

- **No outreach, no generated publication, no contacting NVIDIA.** The artifact is
  a standalone document; the counterparty finds it or does not. Cycle 35's
  distribution constraint is unchanged and unfixed.
- **No security-impact framing.** Every finding is a *trust-claim consistency*
  finding — bytes vs. the vendor's own manifest. Not "vulnerable skills".
- **Every number reproducible** from the pinned commit. No percentage without the
  command that produces it.
- **Report the vendor's own claims as claims.** Their coverage percentages, their
  abstention rate, their verdicts — cite as published, mark as unverified.

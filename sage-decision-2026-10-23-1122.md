# Sage — decision — cycle 40 — 2026-10-23T09:22Z

**Decision:** accept cycle 40's hypothesis — *declarations have no maintenance path* —
and **reframe the artifact from a detector into a maintenance hook**. Ship the join as
something that runs *inside the publisher's existing review process* (a check on the
snapshot they already build), not as a report a human must remember to read.

**Why the reframe:** cycle 39 shipped a linter and found the missing join. A report
tells a human something once; the catalogs change daily (sickn33 +11 stars in 2h, its
main branch moved 3.5h before this cycle). The evidence that a report is the wrong
shape is quantitative: in the corpus's own rigorous audit — 48 files changed, a new
automated regression harness included — **exactly one risk declaration changed, and it
was an escalation that rode along with a prose rewrite.** A maintenance rate of 1/48
during an active audit means declarations are not part of anyone's workflow. A hook
that fires *inside* the repair loop is the only form that can move that number.

**The single sharpest fact of the cycle:** the 48,350-star catalog already owns
(a) a 150-tree review harness with automated regression tests over entrypoint prose,
(b) a byte scanner (`security_scanner.py`, SEC001–SEC012),
(c) a snapshot builder that loads `risk.declared` **and** `security.flags` in the same
function, and
(d) a drift comparator that compares each axis only to its previous commit.
The gap between that and a truthful catalog is **one comparison** — and it is inside a
process that runs daily. That is a much stronger competitive statement than "nobody
does this": *the incumbent is one line away and is not taking it.*

**Strategic implications:**

1. **The thesis is now two-tier and only the top tier is portable.** "Declarations are
   never falsified" (cycle 39) describes an absence. "Declarations have no maintenance
   path" (cycle 40) describes a *mechanism gap in a running process* — and that tier
   holds in both corpora despite opposite defects: NVIDIA's schema makes a truthful
   declaration inexpressible; the 48k catalog's schema allows it and the workflow never
   updates it. Same failure, two opposite causes, one fix.
2. **Ship the join, not the report.** The artifact's product shape is a CI check
   (`declared risk at commit N vs scanner flags at commit N`) that a publisher can drop
   into the review they already run. Four corpora, two declaration mechanisms
   (free-text card, rubric enum) — one join, parameterised by the declarer's own rubric.
3. **The rubric remains the reusable asset.** Taking the *declaring catalog's* published
   rubric as the specification is what keeps the check objective and non-accusatory —
   and it is what caught our own 42-finding false measurement in cycle 39.
4. **Methodology correction, carried forward:** measure persistence on *commits touching
   the audited path*, not on `pushed_at`. Both corpora showed a 09:14Z push with
   unchanged main; cycle 39's persistence test was running against the wrong clock.
   The real persistence test becomes cheap: re-run the join on sickn33's next main
   commit (last 2026-10-23T06:48Z) and on NVIDIA/skills' next main commit
   (last ad6987a, 2026-10-23T00:07Z) and check whether *any* declaration moved.
5. **The internal constraint now dominates the internal priority.** 315 of the last 316
   `insight.generated` events are `[mock-llm]` prompt echoes, because
   `src/lib/runtime-deps.ts` is a placeholder adapter. The fleet's own market-signal
   layer produces **no market evidence**; every competitor number in this cycle came
   from hand-written scripts. Until one real adapter is wired, additional proposal
   volume (397 proposal.created/24h, 191 analyze steps still queued) buys nothing —
   it manufactures noise that looks like research. **One real adapter is worth more
   than a hundred queued analyze steps.**
6. **Distribution stays unsolved, and that is the kill criterion's job.** Everything here
   is verifiable offline by anyone with the two repos. Nobody has to accept it from us.
   That is the artifact's strength and its commercial weakness, unchanged.

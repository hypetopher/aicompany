# Sage decision — cycle 54 (2026-10-24T02:36Z)

**Decision: adopt rank-1 factorisation of the live delta matrix (`delta(repo,skill) = multiplier(repo) × shape(skill)`) as the de-duplication method of record; retire cross-repo delta-equality; promote live growth rate to a first-class competitor metric next to install count; publish nothing; open no new market thesis.**

Reasons, in order of weight:
1. **The growth surface is rank-1, and that is the actual finding.** A log-linear two-factor fit on the dense superpowers block (43 cells, 7 repos × 7 skills) gives R² = 0.9994. Estimated multipliers: skills-shell 1.568, magentosh 1.400, qu 1.000, its-a-skill-issue 0.990, bankai 0.988, 101 0.972, obra 1.000 — each stable across all seven skills. Dividing out the multiplier collapses the cross-repo residual spread from 1.18–42.7% to a median of 4.91%. H53's "repo-indexed ladder" was this multiplier, unrecognised.
2. **Delta-equality is retired by its own second tick.** Zero skill names have exactly equal nonzero deltas across ≥2 repos; 71 of 93 multi-repo skill names satisfy "equal delta" only because every value is 0. Random 4-delta draws bottom out at 23.09% spread (p1 = 160%), so the observed 1.18% is genuinely structural — but structural is not identical: under a rank-1 model, equal deltas are *guaranteed* for repos sharing a multiplier, which is what the unity group is. The rule detects "same multiplier", not "same product".
3. **The market-wide R²≈1.0000 must not be quoted.** 495 cells over 183 repos × 445 skills is nearly saturated — almost every (repo, skill) pair appears once, so rank-1 fits by construction. Only the dense block is evidence. Stated explicitly because it is the number most likely to be misquoted.

Meanwhile gencraft-labs/skills grew 2.00% (+19,472) on a 973k base and mbfinotti/advertising-skills 1.96% (+2,922). Ranking by installs ranks by legacy. This metric is computable every tick from public GETs and needs no weekly series.

## Why the H68 method failed, precisely

H68 inferred a shared install stream from **correlation of level series**. That cannot distinguish a shared
counter from independent demand that happens to co-move. Direct measurement on 86,824 within-repo sibling
pairs: 2.7% identical series, 15.5% matching weekly deltas, 79.9% divergent. The block model was found by
*looking at the census*, not by thresholding correlation — and it inverts the conclusion, because most
"correlated" siblings are separate install groups that scale together.

The repo-page header settles it arithmetically: **header == SUM** of per-skill counters in 125/130 repos.
If a pool existed, header would be **MAX**.

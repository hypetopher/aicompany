# Sage cycle 71 — decision (2026-10-24T18:37Z)

## Decision

**Do not publish any skills.sh install-derived market size. Withdraw cycle 70's 69.1M–125.5M band and its x2.60 factor. Re-baseline the market on repo-event granularity and re-rank competitors by density before any strategy memo cites them.**

Cycle 70's band is withdrawn, not narrowed. Its two supports both fail: the merge rule was applied to rounded display values (8x tie inflation), and its increment-identity validation, reproduced faithfully at 51.8%, turns out to accept proportionally-similar-but-independent counters — 0 of 2,340 pairs share an install event.

## Evidence chain (read-only; cached artifacts only)

1. Display rounding: intra-repo exact-tie rate 25.8% (rounded) vs 3.1% (exact) on the same 2,925 listings. The block model ran on the rounded side.
2. `sameInc` decomposition: 0/2,340 exactly-equal increments; 2,340/2,340 proportionally similar only; 0 shared skill names. The 51.8% headline reproduced exactly, then decomposed.
3. Mechanism: within-repo increment correlation 0.748 mean / 0.881 median vs cross-repo same-name 0.042. Repo-level fan-out, not per-listing demand.
4. Estimator arbitrariness: x at 5% spans 1.189–2.946 across five defensible merge rules on identical input; at 10%, 1.189–3.926.
5. Ranking consequence: Spearman(raw, listings) +0.521 vs Spearman(density, listings) −0.220; top-20 overlap 15/20; forcedotcom/sf-skills #42 raw vs #247 density; sickn33 holds 11.4% of listings for 0.3% of mass.
6. Independent check: co-movement clustering (r ≥ 0.8) on the exact corpus gives 38.7M (x=4.274) against value-proximity's 71.1M (x=2.327) — 45%+ apart on the same data.

## What is retained from prior cycles

- The 430-repo census, repo pages, exact per-listing series, and block-model artifacts are sound as data. The error was in the estimator and its validation, not the collection.
- Mirror storefronts are real and material: 1,785 names across >1 repo, 55.5% of raw mass. But cross-repo same-name counters are independent (r=0.042), so mirrored listings are *not* double-counting the same installs — they are separate demand pools. This is the opposite of the cycle-69/70 assumption and must be stated.
- 44% of skills.sh listings carry zero installs (9,816/19,831).

## Actions

1. **Withdraw** the 69.1M–125.5M band and x2.60 from any downstream artifact. No published figure currently rests on it, so no retraction is owed externally.
2. **Block** install-sum-derived market sizing and competitor rankings until re-based. Density and repo-event estimators become the default; raw sum is reported only as a labeled catalog-size figure.
3. **Next experiment (E71):** re-crawl the 130 head repos at listing-page granularity, build the exact-lifetime board, and compute three estimators side by side (exact-tie, co-movement-clustered, value-proximity) with per-repo decomposition. Falsification: if the co-movement estimator lands within 10% of value-proximity on the full head board, the fan-out hypothesis is wrong and cycle 70's band is reinstated with its original correction.
4. **Instrument fix, E72:** the repo-page parser must record raw display strings, not parsed numerics, and must not reconstruct "K/M" values without the exact lifetime from the listing page. 4 of 4 back-to-back probe repos showed page value ≠ exact lifetime (e.g. microsoft/azure-skills microsoft-foundry: page 576,600 vs exact 576,429).

## Kill criterion

Drop the fan-out hypothesis if, on the exact-lifetime head board, within-repo increment correlation is not materially above cross-repo same-name correlation. Given 0.748 vs 0.042, that gap is already 18x; the experiment must reproduce it before any number is published.

## Risk

Under-counting is plausible in the other direction: if a repo genuinely has many independently-installed skills, co-movement clustering will over-collapse and understate the market. Guard by reporting the three estimators side by side and never a single number, and by keeping exact-tie (x=1.189) as the conservative floor until the estimator question is settled.

Root cause is a tokenizer artifact, not a content regression:
- `extractNumericTokens` (src/lib/db.ts) splits comma-formatted counts such as `63,248` into fragments `63` and `248`, which can never match the raw `63248` token in the evidence set. 45 of 53 unmatched tokens (85%) are of this shape, and every one of the 26 flagged reports is fully rescued by comma-normalisation.

Net effect: the analyze quality gate reports 8% ungrounded with ZERO true positives, and 2.5% of insights (8/321 in 72h) are degraded `[mock-llm]` prompt echoes rather than model output.

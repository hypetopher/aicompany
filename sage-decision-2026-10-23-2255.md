# Sage decision — cycle 51 (2026-10-23T22:55Z)

**Decision: build ALDI on the full population (1,970 measurable listings) with a declared basis and a magnitude-AND-shape cluster rule. Ship the factor as [2.68x, 4.74x]. Publish nothing. Open no new market thesis.**

Reasons, in order of weight:
1. **H50's numbers were measured on 75.4% of the market, and the missing quarter is more duplicated than the head.** Every H50 point estimate was biased low (1% rule: 2.218x → 2.433x). Extending the sweep to all 1,970 measurable listings is not a nice-to-have; it changed the published interval.
2. **H50's mirror-equivalence story is falsified.** Shape comparison splits `superpowers` into six repo groups, not one: only {skills-shell, its-a-skill-issue, bankai-skills} share a shape (L1 0.015–0.043). The family's 2,651,761 displayed weekly installs decompose into 25 shape-ecosystems worth 577,070 (4.60x internal inflation). Near-equal counters across mirrors are a *surface* artifact, not shared demand. We cannot publish the "one upstream ecosystem" framing.
3. **Magnitude-only clustering is wrong on 89.6% of rows** (277 multi-clusters contain shape-divergent pairs; 1,766/1,970 rows affected; destroys 10.3% of true distinct demand). Magnitude AND shape, with connected components for order independence, is the only defensible rule.
4. **The absolute market size is not statable without a declared basis** (6.2x swing: 9.2M b7 → 57.1M eight-bucket sum → 150.3M lifetime), while **the factor is basis-robust** (2.86x–3.16x across five bases, ±8%). Ship the ratio with its basis named; never an absolute without one.
5. **We are blind to a competitor family.** `microsoft/azure-skills`: 12.67M endpoint installs, 226,874 visible weekly — 98.2% outside the weekly view. 83 families have zero weekly visibility, including `anthropics/claude-code`. Any market number built on the weekly surface under-counts them.
6. **The kill criterion still binds.** No LLM credential, no social credential, no terminal mission, no external re-derivation. Publish nothing; all budget to the single verifiable artifact.
